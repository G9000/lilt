# What's new

Lilt grows a little at a time. Here's what changed in each version, newest first. The widget in the
room shows a dot whenever there's a version you haven't read yet.

## 0.2 · 1 November 2026

A big one. The voice course was rewritten and checked against clinical guidance and coaching
practice, Today got smarter about how a practice day flows, and there's a lot more to read aloud.

### The voice course

- **A new course, in three parts.** _Get ready_, _The skills_ and _Bring it together_: thirteen
  chapters, from setting up your mic to phone calls, each teaching one skill with real technique.
  [Open Practice](#/studio)
- **Articulation, and laughs and little sounds.** Two new chapters: light, quick consonants, and
  the laughs, "um"s and "aww"s that happen before you can think about them.
- **Checked, then checked again.** AI-assisted audits went through every line from a clinical point
  of view, as learners following it day by day, and against established programmes. Claims now match
  the evidence, and every card makes sense on its own.
- **Safer by design.** Red flags and when to see a GP or ENT, early signs of overwork, building up
  your voice use slowly, and a page for hard days. No clinician has reviewed the course yet, and it
  says so.

### More to read aloud

- **Extras.** A [Reading room](#/studio/reading-room) of minute-long passages, short
  [Stories](#/studio/stories) told a part at a time, and [Articles](#/studio/articles) to read like a
  presenter. They stay out of Today's cards.
- **Titles and reading times** on every long passage, so the shelves are easy to browse.

### Today

- **Warm-up first, cool-down last.** Your chosen warm-up opens every day, and the cool-down closes
  it. Pick yours in [Practice settings](#/studio/settings).
- **Eyes-off takes.** Some reviews of cards you know well hide the pitch line: you rate by ear first,
  then see what it did. Less feedback while practising helps a skill stick.
- **Before and after.** About six weeks in, and every few weeks after, your first recording comes
  back to play beside your voice now, with three quick questions.
- **It hurts, then resume.** Saying a card hurt pauses everything, and the same page now has the way
  back when you feel fine.
- **Always room for something new.** One place is kept for a new card when reviews pile up, so the
  course never stalls.

### Your pitch, your range

- **A range that moves with you.** When a take sits above your range and felt easy, Lilt suggests
  moving the range up, instead of telling you to come down.
- **The pitch scale, gentler.** It's measured from the overlap between the average voices, folded
  away on daily cards, and you can turn it off.

### Around the app

- **Auto theme.** Light or dark follows your computer, unless you choose one in Settings.
- **A tidier Practice.** Chapters grouped by part (fold the ones you're not using), numbered steps,
  readable names for hums and vowels, and a recorder that stays in view while you speak.
- **A clearer desk.** The dock is named for what it opens: Today, Practice, Recordings and Courses.
- **What's new.** This page, and the little card in the room that points to it.

## 0.1 · 28–31 October 2026

The first version: a small, private place to practise out loud, a few minutes a day.

- **The room and the desk.** An illustrated studio above a city, in morning, day, sunset and night
  light, with a sleeping cat. Sit down at the computer and every part of Lilt is an app on its
  screen.
- **Practice.** Chapters as plain Markdown, an Obsidian-style sidebar, and a recorder under every
  text that draws your pitch live.
- **Today's cards.** Spaced repetition for your voice: say a card, listen back, rate how it felt.
  Ratings are yours; pitch numbers never decide them.
- **Every take kept.** Recordings like voice memos, measured with Praat, compared with your earlier
  takes of the same text, and described in words, never scored.
- **Resonance.** F2 and F3 measured on held vowels and compared with your own first take.
- **Your own version of the course.** Ask your AI, over MCP or ChatGPT, to remix a chapter. Your
  copy keeps its history and undo, and updates from Lilt arrive piece by piece.
- **Little comforts.** A lo-fi radio made live in your browser (or your own Spotify), a sticky note,
  a clock, a plant that grows on the days you practise, and string lights when you're done.
