# Painted corner artwork

`summer.png` is original AI-generated exterior scenery, created with the built-in image-generation tool on 4 November 2026 for this prototype. The user's approved generated room concept supplied the palette and painterly summer mood. No image from the user's reference attachments is shipped.

Prompt: Paint only the exterior landscape seen outside a second-floor bedroom: cyan summer sky, luminous cream clouds, distant blue-gray tiled rooftops and fine utility wires, lush leafy branches at the edges. Hand-painted animation-background appearance, gouache brushwork, fine paper grain, sage greens and aqua blues. No room, frame, text, logo or watermark.

`surfaces.png` is an original AI-generated 2×2 surface atlas made with the same tool on the same date. Prompt: four equal, flat, evenly lit gouache swatches with no labels or objects: warm gray-beige weathered wood, dusty aqua plaster, ivory linen, and muted turquoise enamel. Delicate irregular brushwork, low contrast, no grunge or glossy finish.

Furniture geometry, supplemental surface brush textures, and sunlight patches are authored in the application. The distant landscape is a flat painting behind the modeled window; the desk, lamp, fan, curtains and plants are 3D geometry.
