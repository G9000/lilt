# Painted music players

Generated on 7 November 2026 with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool, not the API CLI.
Exact final prompts are in [prompts.json](prompts.json).

- `clear-day.png`, `clear-morning.png`, `clear-sunset.png`, `clear-night.png`: edits of the
  corresponding Lilt `room-*-v3.webp`, removing only the small portable cassette player.
  The application composites a feathered rectangle around that player, preserving the rest
  of the original room painting and its other interactive objects.
- `cassette.png`: cream cassette player with pink trim and brass handle.
- `boombox.png`: sage boombox with cream trim.
- `radio.png`: walnut radio with a woven speaker grille and amber tuning window.
- `turntable.png`: walnut record player with cream deck and terracotta record label.

The day room painting served as style and perspective reference. All four cutouts have real
transparent alpha (checked outside the silhouette and above the device). The generated files
are copied here unchanged. These are dimensional painted 2D assets. The app adds matching room
lighting, small moving reel/label details and a playback light; the chassis remains still.
