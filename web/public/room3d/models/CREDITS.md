# 3D room models

Some of the furniture in the 3D room test (`#/room-3d`) is from Kenney's Furniture Kit 2.0
(https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit), CC0: free for any use, credit not required but given
here with thanks. `License.txt` is Kenney's own. Only the models the room uses are kept: the bed,
the desk chair, the screens, keyboard and mouse, speakers, drawers and cabinets, pillows, books and
small plants. The room scales them (the kit is drawn at roughly half size) and redraws them with
toon shading. Everything else in the room is built from simple shapes in `web/src/room3d`.
