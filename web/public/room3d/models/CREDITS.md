# 3D room models

The furniture in the 3D room test (`#/room-3d`) is from Kenney's Furniture Kit 2.0
(https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit), CC0: free for any use, credit not required but given
here with thanks. `License.txt` is Kenney's own. Only the models the room uses are kept; the room
scales them about 1.9× (the kit is drawn at roughly half size) and redraws them with toon shading.
