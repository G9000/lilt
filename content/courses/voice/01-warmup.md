NHS Fife: humming gently on a comfortable pitch, and gentle pitch glides as a warm-up.

## hum-comfortable

mmmmmmm

> Hum gently on a comfortable note for a few seconds. Notice where you feel it.

## hum-forward

mmmmmmm

> Hum again, aiming the sound forward until your lips tingle.

## glide

mmm ↗ ↘

> A gentle glide: slide a little up and back down, like a soft siren. Easy, never strained.

## hum-vowels

mmmee · mmmaa · mmmoo

> Start with the hum and let it open into each vowel.
