Resonance is how the space above your voice box shapes the sound: a smaller, brighter space sounds lighter, a bigger one darker. Lilt measures it from two resonances of each vowel (F2 and F3) and compares every take with your first take of the same vowel. Keep the rest the same each time: an easy pitch, the same distance from the same microphone. Then only the space changes. It's a rough guide; your ear decides.

## ee

eeeeee

> Hold a comfortable "ee", as in "see", for about four seconds, on the same easy pitch each time.

## ah

aaaaaa

> Hold an open "ah", as in "father", for about four seconds.

## oo

oooooo

> Hold an "oo", as in "food", for about four seconds. It's the darkest vowel, so small changes show here.
