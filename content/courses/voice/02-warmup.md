Stretch before the run. Nobody likes being woken up with a foghorn.

**Breath.** Hand on your belly; slow breaths in through the nose, three to five.

**Release.** Big-apple mouth, silent misty-mirror breath, eyebrows up.

**Hum easy.** Clear and smooth, never breathy or pushed.

**Signs it's working.** Smooth and effortless. Anything strains? Smaller and quieter.

## hum

mmmmmmm

> Your check-in: hum on a comfortable note for a few seconds. Notice where you feel the buzz.

## lip-trill

brrrrrrr

> Let your lips flutter loosely, like a horse or a motorboat. You will look silly. That's the point.

## ng

nnnnggg

> Hum on "ng", as at the end of "sing". Feel it buzz behind your nose.

## zzz

zzzzzz · vvvvvv

> A gentle buzzing "zzz", then "vvv", on one easy note.

## siren

mmm ↗ ↘

> A soft siren: slide a little up and back down. Ambulance, not air-raid. Never further than feels easy.
