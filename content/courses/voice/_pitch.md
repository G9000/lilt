Average speaking pitch from Leung, Oates, Papp and Chan (2022), "Speaking fundamental frequencies of adult speakers of Australian English and effects of sex, age, and geographical location", Journal of Voice 36(3), 434.e1, as listed in the University of Sydney Voice Lab's normative reference sheet: men 115 Hz (SD 21), women 199 Hz (SD 28), reading aloud, from Australian English adults. The ranges shown around each average are plus or minus one standard deviation, not the full range of women's or men's voices. The 145–165 Hz band is a historical reference from Oates and Dacakis (1983), not a universal boundary: later studies and guidance give different ranges. These population references do not determine your gender, predict how everyone will hear you, or set a training target. Pitch, resonance, voice quality and expression work together; their relative contributions depend on the speaker, listener, language and task. You can hide this scale in Practice settings.
