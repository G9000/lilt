The melody of your voice. Monotone gives NPC reading the terms and conditions.

**Three ways.** Read a line plainly, as a storyteller, then gently.

**Lift what matters.** The important words, and the end of questions.

**Over-act.** Real life tones it down for you.

**Signs it's working.** Your pitch line waves instead of flatlining.

## melody

It was the first warm morning of spring, and the whole street seemed to wake up at once.

> Your check-in: read it with a gentle, natural melody. Let it lift and fall.

## storyteller

Once upon a time, a tiny cat found a door that had never been opened before.

> Kids' story-time energy: big rises and falls. Commit to the bit.

## gentle-news

I'm so sorry, the café is closed today, but they're open again tomorrow.

> Gently, as if giving someone a little bad news. Soft, caring melody.

## question

Oh, really? Are you coming too?

> Let each question lift at the end.

## emphasis

I didn't say she took it. I said she borrowed it.

> Lift the words that matter: "say", then "borrowed".

## exaggerate

Could I get a coffee, please? Thank you so much!

> Exaggerate the rise and fall, like you're slightly over-acting. Then try it again, a bit more natural.
