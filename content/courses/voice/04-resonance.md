How the space above your voice box shapes the sound. Lowkey the main character of this course.

**Find the buzz.** Hum and touch your chest, then your face. You want it forward, on your lips.

**Make it bright.** Smile, tongue forward, the tiny lift of a stifled giggle.

**Same everything.** Same pitch, mic and distance on the held vowels, so only the space changes.

**Signs it's working.** Your lips tingle and "ee" sounds bright. Lilt compares each vowel with your first.

## ee

eeeeee

> Your check-in: hold a comfortable "ee", as in "see", for about four seconds, on the same easy pitch each time.

## forward-hum

mmmmmmm

> Hum and aim the sound forward until your lips tingle. A small smile, or starting with a soft "h", helps.

## m-vowels

mmmee · mmmaa · mmmoo

> Start with the forward hum and let it open into each vowel. Keep the buzz up front.

## m-words

many · maybe · mellow · morning · lemon

> Hold the m a little longer at first, then let the word follow, still buzzing forward.

## giggle

eye · air · ear

> Start each word with a tiny stifled giggle, like reading the group chat in a meeting. Feel the space get smaller.

## ah

aaaaaa

> Hold an open "ah", as in "father", for about four seconds.

## oo

oooooo

> Hold an "oo", as in "food", for about four seconds. It's the darkest vowel, so small changes show here.
