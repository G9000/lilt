Lilt's choice: sentences rich in voiced sounds (m, n, l, w and vowels) keep the voice going without breaks, which makes it easier to hear and keep your resonance. This is reading material, not a separate technique.

## s1

My mother made lemon meringue on Monday morning.

> Keep the buzz forward on every m.

## s2

We were walking along the river in the evening.

> One smooth line from start to finish.

## s3

Emily loves warm rolls with honey and lime.

> Light and easy.

## s4

I'm meeting Lena near the main library at noon.

> Natural rise and fall.

## s5

All along the lane, yellow leaves were swirling in the wind.

> Let it flow.

## s6

Many lovely melodies are made of only a few notes.

> Notice the m's and l's.
