Short things you actually say, in a voice that feels like you.

**Make them yours.** Swap in the phrases you really use.

**Your name.** The sneakiest one. Practise it too.

**Signs it's working.** Friendly and natural, not customer-service bot.

## coffee

Hi, could I get a coffee, please?

> Your check-in: friendly and unhurried, like at the counter. Not like the barista owes you money.

## black-coffee

Black coffee, please.

> Let the rise and fall follow the meaning.

## free-later

Hi, it's me! Are you free later?

> Friendly and natural, not performed.

## good-morning

Good morning. How are you today?

> A real greeting, as if someone is there.

## thank-you

Thank you so much, that's lovely.

> Warm, without forcing it higher.

## intro

Hi, I'm Alex. It's really nice to meet you.

> Say your own name instead of Alex. Let it sound warm, not rushed.
