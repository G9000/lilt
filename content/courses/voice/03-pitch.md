How high or low your voice sits. Comfy beats high: a strained squeak sounds like a cartoon mouse with a mortgage.

**Find your note.** Hum, glide up to a higher note that still feels comfy, glide back.

**Your target.** A little above where you started. Never a squeeze.

**Pair it with resonance.** High pitch with a dark sound reads as cartoon. Grow them together.

**Signs it's working.** You can count on it and still feel relaxed. Save your range in [Practice settings](#/studio/settings).

## count-5

One, two, three, four, five.

> Your check-in: count on your target pitch, easy and steady.

## hum-speak

mmm

> Hum at the pitch you'd normally speak at. That's your starting point.

## glide

mmm ↗ ↘

> Glide smoothly up to a higher note that still feels comfy, then back down. We're not trying to summon dogs.

## hum-target

mmmmmm

> Hum a little above your starting point, on the note you liked. Smile slightly; notice the small lift in your throat.

## hum-into-count

mmm… one, two, three, four, five.

> Start with the hum on your target, then let it open into counting on the same note.

## count-20

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty.

> Longer: keep the same easy pitch all the way to twenty. Breathe whenever you need to.
