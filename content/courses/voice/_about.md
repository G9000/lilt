One cue at a time, then a real phrase. Listen back and keep what feels easy and sounds like you. It should never hurt: if your voice feels strained, scratchy or hoarse, stop and rest. Hoarseness that lasts more than about three weeks, sudden voice loss, pain, or a lump in your neck needs a GP or primary care doctor (in the US, ask for an ENT or laryngologist), who can have your vocal folds looked at. After voice surgery, follow your surgeon's rest plan and get the all-clear before these exercises, including whispering. No clinician has reviewed this course yet.
