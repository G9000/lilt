One cue at a time, then a real phrase. Listen back and keep what feels easy and sounds like you. It should never hurt: if your voice feels strained, scratchy or hoarse, stop and rest. Hoarseness that lasts more than about three weeks, sudden voice loss, pain, or a lump in your neck needs a GP, who can refer you to ENT to look at your vocal folds.
