Gentle, daily practice for a lighter, brighter voice that still sounds like you: hear an example, explore one small cue, record, listen
and bring it into an everyday phrase from the first warm-up visit. The chapters cover setup and
warm-ups, then pitch, resonance, vocal weight, articulation and intonation, then everyday phrases,
reading, your own words, laughs and little sounds, and real life.
