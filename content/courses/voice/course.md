Gentle, daily practice for a lighter, brighter voice that still sounds like you: watch a demonstration where a chapter has one, explore one small cue, record, listen
and bring it into an everyday phrase from the first warm-up visit. The chapters cover setup and
warm-ups, then pitch, resonance, vocal weight, articulation and intonation, then everyday phrases,
reading, your own words, laughs and little sounds, being heard, and real life. Going lower has its own chapters for a deeper, darker, fuller voice.
