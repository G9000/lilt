Gentle, daily voice feminization practice: hear an example, explore one small cue, record, listen
and bring it into an everyday phrase from the first warm-up visit. The chapters cover setup,
warm-ups, pitch, resonance, vocal weight and intonation, then longer reading, your own words and real life.
