# Courses

Each folder here is one course: Markdown you can edit in any editor or open as an Obsidian vault.

- `course.md` names the course: `id` (the folder's name), `title`, `kind` (the kind of practice;
  `voice` today), `version`, `source` (where updates will come from, optional) and `locked` (the
  `## ` sections an AI may never change in someone's copy, comma-separated). Its text says what the
  course is for.
- `index.md` is the course's home page; `_about.md` its version and closing note; each
  `NN-name.md` a chapter, in file-name order. `images/` and `audio/` hold its pictures and example
  recordings. See `voice/README.md` for the chapter format.

The folders here are the originals. Someone's own copy of a course, made by their AI, lives in their
data folder (`<data>/courses/<id>`), with a history of changes, undo, and updates from the original.

Voice practice is the course the app shows today.
