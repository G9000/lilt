NHS Fife progression: start with speech you don't have to think about (counting, days, months), so you can focus on how you sound.

## count-1-10

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten.

> An easy pace. Same voice from start to finish.

## days

Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.

> Keep each day in your practised voice.

## months

January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.

> Twelve in a row: notice if the voice drifts by the end.

## count-50-75

Fifty, fifty-one, fifty-two … all the way to seventy-five.

> Longer: take a breath whenever you need one.
