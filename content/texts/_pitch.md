Average speaking pitch from Leung, Oates, Papp and Chan (2022), "Speaking fundamental frequencies of adult speakers of Australian English and effects of sex, age, and geographical location", Journal of Voice 36(3), 434.e1, as listed in the University of Sydney Voice Lab's normative reference sheet: men 115 Hz (SD 21), women 199 Hz (SD 28). The ranges shown around each average are plus or minus one standard deviation. The overlap, where pitch alone can be heard as either, is the "gender ambiguous range" of Oates and Dacakis (1983), 145 to 165 Hz; later listener studies suggest it is wider. Pitch is one part of how a voice is heard: resonance, intonation and how you shape words matter as much.
