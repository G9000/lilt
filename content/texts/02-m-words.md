NHS Fife: short words starting with m, holding the m a little longer at first. You can make up your own.

## m1

meet · many · maybe · Mary · magic

> Hold the m a little longer at first.

## m2

moon · mellow · mild · morning · mine

> Feel the m on your lips before the vowel.

## n1

name · nine · near · noon · new

> The n buzzes just behind your top teeth.

## m3

mini · money · meaning · mimic · mango

> Two m's in some words: keep both forward.
