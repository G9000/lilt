Lilt's choice: lines from the moments you'll really use your voice, so your practice voice comes along to the coffee shop, the phone and the first hello. This is reading material, not a separate technique: say each one the way you would there, then try it again without looking.

## coffee

Hi! Could I get an oat flat white, please? Small is fine.

> Friendly and unhurried, the way you'd say it at the counter.

## phone

Hello, this is Sam speaking. How can I help?

> Answering the phone: keep the "hello" as light as the rest.

## intro

Hi, I'm Alex. It's really nice to meet you.

> Let your name sound warm, not rushed.

## booking

I'd like to book an appointment for Thursday afternoon, if you have one.

> Polite and clear, like talking to a receptionist.

## directions

Sorry, could you tell me how to get to the station from here?

> A real question: let it lift a little at the end.

## thanks

Thank you so much, you've been really helpful. Have a lovely day!

> Bright and easy all the way to the last word.
