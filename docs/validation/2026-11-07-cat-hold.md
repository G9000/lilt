# Click to pet, hold to choose

Verified on Windows on 7 November 2026. A short click or tap pets the cat. A 650ms primary
pointer hold opens its compact picker, without petting on release. The whole-cat outline,
five saved choices and Make it yours entry remain. Enter and Space pet the focused cat.

The hold cancels on movement over 10 screen pixels, Escape, pointer cancellation, lost
capture, blur, hidden document, entering the desk or unmount. Touch release can target the
new modal, so its trailing click is consumed; a fresh dialog press works normally.

## Evidence

- Updated `room-cats.mjs` first failed against the former click-to-open interaction.
- All five cats passed short-click petting, hold-to-open, release suppression and outlines.
  Keyboard petting and modal focus return passed. Drag, Escape, pointer cancellation and
  blur neither opened choices nor petted on release.
- Chrome touch events passed tap-to-pet, hold-to-open and release without petting or dismissal.
